# Cadence

Cadence sells video courses to learners. This glossary covers the language around getting money back after a purchase.

## Language

### Refunds

**Admin**:
A member of Cadence's own staff with the authority to decide Refund Requests.
_Avoid_: The team, Staff

**Team**:
A group of people whose seats for a course were bought together on one purchase.

**Team Manager**:
The person who buys and looks after seats for a Team.
_Avoid_: Admin, Team Admin

**Team Purchase**:
A purchase of several seats in a course for a Team.

**Claimed Seat**:
A seat on a Team Purchase that someone has already taken.

**Refund Request**:
A learner's ask, with their reason, to get back what they paid for a purchase. An Admin approves or declines it. One purchase can have several Refund Requests over time, but only one can be undecided at once, and none can follow an Approved Refund Request.
_Avoid_: Refund (for the ask itself), Cancellation, Reversal, Chargeback

**Open Refund Request**:
A Refund Request no Admin has decided yet.
_Avoid_: Pending, Requested, Awaiting review

**Approved Refund Request**:
A Refund Request an Admin has accepted. Approving it is what leads to a Refund.

**Declined Refund Request**:
A Refund Request an Admin has turned down, with a reason the learner can see. It has no Refund, and the learner may raise a new Refund Request.

**Refund**:
The money returned to a learner after their Refund Request is approved. It is always the full amount they paid. A declined Refund Request has no Refund.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Chargeback, Cancellation

**Pending Refund**:
A Refund an Admin has approved where the money hasn't been confirmed as sent yet.
_Avoid_: Pending (for a Refund Request awaiting review)

**Issued Refund**:
A Refund where the money has been confirmed as sent back to the learner.
_Avoid_: Completed, Paid out

**Failed Refund**:
A Refund where the money couldn't be sent. The Refund Request stays approved, because the decision hasn't changed.
